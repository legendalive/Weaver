# A Ruby Polished by Thorns (Book 1)
### Official Canonical Project Bible & World Compendium
**Tagline:** *The grave gave her a name, blood gave her another. Some legends are born. Others are buried. She was both.*

---
## Logline
From exile she rises: Cecilia Amelia of House Devaroux, believed murdered as an infant, grows up as an ordinary schoolgirl in Nairobi, only to discover she is the secret heir of a slaughtered vampire dynasty and the target of a centuries-old blood vendetta.

## Timeline Overview
Spans ~18 years: Begins at the midnight birth of Cecilia Devaroux and fall of Jose Devaroux at Suburban Hospital (Elowen/Bethesda); 6 years in exile in Valombre, France ending in the burning of Notre-Brume; 12 years in exile in Nairobi, Kenya where Cecilia lives as 'Ruby', culminating in her awakening, the slaying of Thomas Thorn, and return to France.

## I. World Rules & Metaphysics

### Blood Consumption, Solar Walking & Reproduction [The Sun & Reproduction]
**Summary:** Vampires consume blood to be able to walk in the sun and to reproduce.

**Full Rule & Mechanics:**
Vampiric unlife requires blood not merely for energy, but as the metaphysical fuel that anchors cellular vitality. Specifically, vampires must consume blood to be able to walk in the sun without immolating, and blood consumption is the biological and eldritch prerequisite that enables vampires to reproduce and sire offspring. Without regular blood intake, the sun scourges them, their regenerative powers wither, and their reproductive vitality is rendered barren.

**Canonical Evidence & Source:** Mandated core world rule: Vampires walk openly in daylight in Provence and attend schools/markets in Nairobi under the equatorial sun by regularly feeding on blood; Jose and Inesia reproduced blood-born heirs (Mikeh, Daffy, Cecilia) through their potent vitae.

### Blood-Born Vampires vs. The Turned [Biology & Sustenance]
**Summary:** A strict metaphysical caste system separates born vampires from mortal converts.

**Full Rule & Mechanics:**
Blood-Born vampires (such as the Devaroux, Thorns, and Amelias) inherit their dark gift directly through lineage dating back toward the Sires. They possess dense, heavy auras, natural regenerative vitality, and innate combat grace. Turned vampires are mortals converted through the rite of draining mortal blood to the edge of death and infusing the sire's potent vitae. Turned vampires have thinner, flickering auras ('mongrels' to elder houses) but retain mortal ingenuity and adaptability, as demonstrated by Elizabeth Niazi.

**Canonical Evidence & Source:** Chapter 8 notes: 'In stark contrast to the blood-born... stood the turned; those pitiable and often despised souls who were not born into night, but dragged into it... their auras vague and flickering like the light of a dying taper.' Chapter 19 details Liz's turning by Mikeh.

### Immortal Longevity & Calcification [Biology & Sustenance]
**Summary:** Vampires do not rot or decay with age; their tissues and powers calcify and deepen.

**Full Rule & Mechanics:**
Vampires do not die of old age. Their sinews do not wither, nor do their minds dim. Instead of decomposing, their physical forms 'calcify' over centuries, hardening against damage while their connection to primal eldritch energies deepens. Senses sharpen to extreme acuity, physical durability increases, and their auras gain crushing gravitational mass.

**Canonical Evidence & Source:** Chapter 8 states: 'They did not rot with age. Their sinews did not wither. Their thoughts did not dim. Nay, with each century, their strength grew deeper... They did not decay. They calcified.'

### Auras, Psychic Resonance & Dominant Suppression [Auras & Psychic Arts]
**Summary:** Auras broadcast identity and emotional state, but can be masked, pulsed, or suppressed by elders.

**Full Rule & Mechanics:**
Every vampire emits a distinct psychic aura that kindred can detect across great distances. Auras reflect lineage, intent, rage, or grief. Masters can enter 'psychic silence' to disguise themselves as ordinary mortals. Powerful dominant elders (such as Inesia or Thomas) can exert their will to actively suppress the burgeoning auras and supernatural reflexes of younger or turned kindred. Auras can also be pulsed outward across an entire city as an emergency signal to summoned kin.

**Canonical Evidence & Source:** Chapter 10: Ines suppresses Ruby's burgeoning vampirism for 12 years; Chapter 12: Mikeh pulses his aura across Nairobi to alert his scattered family; Chapter 8: Thomas notes a dominant elder can curb volatile aspects of younger vampires.

### Methods of True Death & Willful Penance [Mortality & True Death]
**Summary:** True death occurs only by a stake to the heart, decapitation, or total fire combustion.

**Full Rule & Mechanics:**
Vampires heal rapidly from ordinary mortal trauma, bullet wounds, lacerations, and broken bones. True death can be inflicted only through three definitive methods: 1) A stake through the heart (instantly shattering the core vitae and collapsing the vampire into dust or corpse); 2) Decapitation (severing head from body); 3) Complete combustion by fire. In addition, ancient specialized venoms (such as Zaria's reed-dart) can severely burn and disable immortal tissue. Furthermore, vampires can consciously refuse their blood's innate healing to suffer physical agony as moral penance, as Mikeh did for twelve years.

**Canonical Evidence & Source:** Chapter 1: Tom decapitates the nurse; Thomas stakes Jose through the heart; Chapter 5: Notre-Brume's fire incinerates vampires; Chapter 7: Mikeh willfully refuses to heal his burns; Chapter 20: Cecilia stakes Thomas through the heart.

### The Thirst & Enhanced Senses [Biology & Sustenance]
**Summary:** The Thirst is a cellular, spiritual void demanding constant, disciplined mastery.

**Full Rule & Mechanics:**
Upon transformation, the mortal pulse ceases entirely. Senses expand violently—candlelight blazes like torches, fabrics rustle like thunder, and emotional signatures become tangible odors. The Thirst is not simple dehydration, but an insatiable cellular hunger for mortal vitae that must be governed through iron discipline, lest the vampire degrade into a mindless beast.

**Canonical Evidence & Source:** Chapter 19 documents Liz's awakening: her heartbeat ceases, sounds become deafening, colors hyper-vibrant, and a gnawing spiritual void cries out for vitae, pacified by Mikeh sharing his own blood.

### Hereditary Vampire Hunter Lineage [Vampire Hunters]
**Summary:** Specialized mortal bloodlines carry instinctual muscle memory and lethal anatomical anti-vampire techniques.

**Full Rule & Mechanics:**
Certain human bloodlines were historically bred and trained as holy butchers to purge vampires. Descendants inherit dormant instinctual reflexes, supernatural spatial awareness against vampires, and the ability to turn a vampire's superior momentum and strength against itself using minimal weapons. This genetic memory can awaken under extreme stress even if the individual grew up completely unaware of their lineage.

**Canonical Evidence & Source:** Chapter 20: Andrew Tajiri, an art and photography student, single-handedly slaughters 5-6 attacking vampires at the doorway using only a letter opener, displaying surgical anatomical precision that Ines immediately recognizes as the hallmark of an ancient Hunter clan.

### The Law of Tooth and Claw & The Public Pretext [Law & Politics]
**Summary:** Vampire society has no formal parliaments; rule is by brute power, yet requires righteous pretexts.

**Full Rule & Mechanics:**
Vampires live under no written charters or councils; rank is determined by raw strength, ancient pedigree, and cunning. However, two universal unwritten taboos govern the kindred: 1) Humans must not be harmed needlessly (to avoid exposure and chaos); 2) Overt war against peer houses requires a righteous public pretext (e.g., blood debt or 'a daughter for a daughter'). A lord who attacks peers without a legitimate casus belli is branded a tyrant, risking intervention by primordial neutral clans like House Adelyde.

**Canonical Evidence & Source:** Chapter 1 explains the pecking order of tooth and claw; Chapter 8 details Thomas Thorn's political frustration after Tom prematurely kills Cecilia, stripping him of the public 'daughter for a daughter' justification.

## II. Houses & Dynasties

### House Devaroux (Exiled / Reclaiming Throne)
- **Sigil / Emblem:** Nightingale singing with wings wide and free (carved on ancient signet rings)
- **Philosophy / Motto:** Honor, ancestral restraint, protection of the vulnerable, and vows unbroken by sword or flame.
- **Seat of Power:** Devaroux Estate, Elowen (Bethesda, Maryland, along the Whispering Current / Potomac River)
- **Bloodline Potency:** Direct ancient descent from the fabled Sires; thick ancestral fire, natural commanding aura, near-instant cellular regeneration.
- **Notable Members:** Jose Devaroux (Patriarch, Deceased), Inesia 'Ines' Amelia Devaroux (Matriarch), Mikeh 'Michael' Devaroux (Firstborn Son & Heir), Daffy Devaroux (Elder Daughter), Cecilia Amelia Devaroux / Ruby (Youngest Daughter & Avenging Heir), Elizabeth 'Liz' Niazi (Turned Childe / Bonded to Mikeh)

One of the most ancient, feared, and revered vampire bloodlines on earth. Under Patriarch Jose Devaroux, the family ruled Elowen with quiet dignity and unprecedented restraint, defending lesser clans and observing the sacred law that mortals not be harmed needlessly. Following Jose's murder, the house endured 18 years of hunted diaspora before the heir Cecilia awakened to slay their usurper.

### House Amelia (Decimated / Vanguard Remnant)
- **Sigil / Emblem:** Twin blades and storm crest
- **Philosophy / Motto:** Endurance through sorrow; steadfast loyalty unto death; blades unsheathed for kin.
- **Seat of Power:** Old World enclaves; blood-bound stronghold allied with Devaroux in Elowen
- **Bloodline Potency:** Warriors born of sorrow and trained in war; unyielding mental discipline, flawless bladecraft, stealth, and tactile psychic shielding.
- **Notable Members:** Inesia Amelia Devaroux, Cass of House Amelia, Selene of House Amelia

The maternal lineage of Inesia and sworn blood-allies to House Devaroux. When Thomas Thorn attacked the Devaroux Estate, the Amelias formed the vanguard living shield and were near obliterated. The sole battle-scarred survivors, Cass and Selene, accompanied Inesia and her children into exile as unshakeable protectors.

### House Thorn (Shattered / Usurper Fallen)
- **Sigil / Emblem:** The Thorn's sting (used in black-wax seal ciphers)
- **Philosophy / Motto:** Cunning is the coin of the realm, muscle is the seal. Dominion through fear; a daughter for a daughter.
- **Seat of Power:** Usurped Devaroux Estate, Elowen (ruled for 12+ years)
- **Bloodline Potency:** Primeval eldritch force; ancient obsidian psychic presence, cold calculating malice, mental suppression.
- **Notable Members:** Thomas Thorn (Patriarch, Slain), The Deceased Eldest Daughter (Slain by Jose), Tom Thorn (Son & Heir, Wounded/Fled), Zaria Thorn (Youngest Daughter, Exiled)

An ancient, sprawling dominion characterized by patient venom and cold conquest. Led by Thomas Thorn, the house overthrew Jose Devaroux after Jose slew Thomas's favorite cruel daughter. Thomas consolidated absolute power across Elowen until Cecilia staked him in Ines's garden. With Thomas slain and Tom crippled, surviving daughter Zaria is in foreign exile.

### House Hagen (Compromised / Civil Fratricide)
- **Sigil / Emblem:** Frost and wind of the northern wastes
- **Philosophy / Motto:** Pragmatism above passion; survive by bending to the prevailing wind.
- **Seat of Power:** Northern keeps / Cold northern wastes
- **Bloodline Potency:** Lesser ancient house; hardy constitution, heavy brutal combat blades, cold tactical maneuvering.
- **Notable Members:** Lord Hagen (Patriarch, Slain by Jone), Jone, Son of Hagen (Heir, Broken by Guilt), Aunt Maeve (Perceptive clan elder)

Descended from the frigid northern reaches. Former allies of the Devaroux who bent the knee to Thomas Thorn out of fear and opportunistic self-preservation. Lord Hagen spied on his own son Jone's romance with Daffy Devaroux to curry favor with Tom Thorn, culminating in Jone impaling Lord Hagen in the back during the climactic garden raid.

### House Adelyde (Reclusive Ancient Watchers)
- **Sigil / Emblem:** Mountain roots and silent ice
- **Philosophy / Motto:** Neutrality, isolation, primordial law, and merciless judgment against blasphemy.
- **Seat of Power:** Adelyde Estate, deep in the mountain foothills bordering Elowen
- **Bloodline Potency:** The most primordial and formidable house in existence; older than Thorn and Devaroux, possessing cataclysmic elder authority.
- **Notable Members:** Raphael (Ancient Patriarch), Silas (Legendary primordial warrior of the old days)

An enigmatic elder clan that disdains nocturnal court politics. Even Thomas Thorn dared not provoke their silent gaze. When Andrew Tajiri (a hereditary vampire hunter) is revealed to be Cecilia's lover and integrated into the Devaroux circle, House Adelyde marks the union as ancient heresy, forcing Cecilia and Andy into foreign exile to avoid their wrath.

### The Sires / First-Born (Mythic Progenitors)
- **Sigil / Emblem:** The First Darkness
- **Philosophy / Motto:** The primordial origin of the dark gift.
- **Seat of Power:** Ancient forgotten rituals and the dawn of history
- **Bloodline Potency:** Source of all vampiric vitae and eldritch resonance.
- **Notable Members:** Ancient Patriarchs and Matriarchs of origin

The fabled first-born of the dark gift whose names are lost to time or spoken only in dread ritual. The closer a vampire bloodline traces to the Sires, the heavier their psychic aura and physical majesty.

### Mortals & Vampire Hunter Bloodlines (Co-Existing in Ignorance / Ancient Hunters Active)
- **Sigil / Emblem:** The Hunter's instincts and surgeon-like bladecraft
- **Philosophy / Motto:** Unknowing masses bustle in everyday commerce; ancient hunters exist to purge the night.
- **Seat of Power:** Global human civilization (Bethesda, Provence, Nairobi)
- **Bloodline Potency:** Hereditary hunters possess instinctual muscle memory, supernatural leverage against vampire anatomy, and ruthless surgical reflexes.
- **Notable Members:** Andrew 'Andy' Tajiri (Hereditary Hunter), Sarah Tajiri (Andy's missing sister), Elias (Thomas Thorn's mortal steward of 50 years), The Attending Nurse at Suburban Hospital, The Celestial Mechanics Scholar

While the vast majority of mankind lives blind to the hidden war of the kindred, rare mortal lineages were bred as specialized vampire hunters. Andrew Tajiri carries this bloodline unaware until his instincts take over during the garden assault.

### Elowen (Bethesda, Maryland) (Mortal Name: Bethesda, Montgomery County, Maryland, USA)

- **Significance:** Ancestral capital and primary seat of North American vampire power.

To mortals, Bethesda is an affluent, peaceful suburb along the Potomac River with murmured streams and humming commerce. In the old tongue of the kindred, it is Elowen—an ancient domain where forgotten bloodlines have dwelt since before written history. Borders the Whispering Current (Nation's River / Potomac) and contains hidden underground tunnels, ancient estates, and contested nocturnal courts.

A sprawling estate with Gothic spires piercing the heavens, stained-glass galleries, cavernous libraries, and ancient underground tunnels running beneath the foundations. Cold and austere during Thorn's occupation, it is re-occupied by Mikeh and Daffy following Thomas's fall.
