The tour

Every tool a novel needs. None it doesn't.

Nine rooms in the studio, each built for one job in the life of a book.

Write

Manuscript & Main Novel

Draft freely in the Manuscript; finalize cleanly into the Main Novel. One source of truth, always — no duplicated chapters, no version archaeology.

Remember

Config Bible

Characters, places, world rules, prose style and AI instructions in one living reference — the contract every generation must obey.

Plan

Plot Builder

Arcs, beats and dependencies at a glance, so structure problems surface in planning, not in draft three.

Draft

Scribe

AI drafting in your voice and canon, with accept, rewrite and discard on every block. You stay the author; it stays the assistant.

Revise

Recheck & return

Pull a finished passage back to the draft, rewrite it, and send it home to the exact spot it came from.

Series

Series & shared bibles

One canon across every book in a series, with new books added any time — the bible persists, the story grows.

Capture

Notes

A scratchpad one tap or shortcut away, for the idea that arrives mid-sentence. It saves, syncs, and gets out of your way.

Protect

Cloud Vault

Compressed sync and backup across your devices, with automatic saving as you write. Pro keeps every device in step.

Ship

Cover Studio & Export

Generate cover concepts, upload your artwork, place the author name exactly where you want it — then export .docx, .txt or a market-ready EPUB with the cover embedded.
