Questions writers ask first

What happens to my books if I cancel Pro?

Nothing is deleted. Your manuscripts stay on your device and in any exports you've made; the cloud vault holds your backup while Pro is active, and you can re-sync any time you return.

Does the AI read or train on my manuscript?

Generations use your bible and the passage you're working on, sent over encrypted connections to providers that don't train on customer content. Your book is never added to a model.

Can I start free and upgrade mid-book?

Yes — most writers do. Your projects, bibles and notes carry over exactly as they are the moment Pro activates.

How fast does Pro activate after payment?

Activation is handled by the Weaver team, usually within one business day. Use your Weaver account email at checkout so it lands on the right account.

What payment methods work?

Gumroad checkout accepts major cards and PayPal, and handles sales tax and invoicing on your behalf.
